How it is calculated

Teams that almost never lose at home. Measured on their last 15 home league games.

Fortress looks at each team's last 15 home league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 80%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of Fortress (93%+) came true the next time in 82% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
