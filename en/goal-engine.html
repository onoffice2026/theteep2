How it is calculated

Teams whose matches almost always bring goals. Share of their last 15 league games with 2+ goals.

GoalEngine looks at each team's last 15 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 86%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of GoalEngine (93%+) came true the next time in 80% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
