How it is calculated

Teams that take the lead early and keep it. Share of their last 10 league games led at half-time and won.

FrontRunner looks at each team's last 10 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 50%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of FrontRunner (50%+) came true the next time in 33% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
