How it is calculated

Teams whose games are open at both ends. Share of their last 10 league games in which both teams scored.

OpenDoors looks at each team's last 10 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 80%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of OpenDoors (80%+) came true the next time in 58% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
