How it is calculated

Teams that beat the bottom half of their league again and again. Their record against the lower half of the table.

TheCrusher looks at each team's last 15 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 70%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of TheCrusher (80%+) came true the next time in 70% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
