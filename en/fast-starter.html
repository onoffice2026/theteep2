How it is calculated

Teams that rarely wait for the second half. Share of their last 10 league games with a goal of their own before the break.

FastStarter looks at each team's last 10 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 70%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of FastStarter (70%+) came true the next time in 54% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
