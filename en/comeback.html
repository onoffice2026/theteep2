How it is calculated

Teams that do not give up. When they were behind at half-time, how often they still avoided defeat (last 20 league games).

Comeback looks at each team's last 20 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 66%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of Comeback (67%+) came true the next time in 66% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
