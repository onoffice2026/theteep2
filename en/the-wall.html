How it is calculated

Teams that rarely concede more than one goal. Measured on their last 15 league games.

TheWall looks at each team's last 15 league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 75%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of TheWall (93%+) came true the next time in 76% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
