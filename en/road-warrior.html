How it is calculated

Teams that travel well. Share of their last 8 away league games without a defeat.

RoadWarrior looks at each team's last 8 away league games measured. A team enters the list when its value is at least 85%. In our archive test (league matches since July 2022), teams in the top band of RoadWarrior (88%+) came true the next time in 69% of cases. The values are recalculated every few hours, only from games already played.
